I'm Ching Yu Dai — Emma to most people — an artist and maker who moves between painting, drawing, and three-dimensional sculpture.

My work explores memory, perspective, and the emotional weight of everyday spaces. I collect color, texture, city scenes, and small everyday details, then turn them into pieces that hold a bit of that feeling. I'm drawn to expressive color, intricate detail, and materials that can carry a story of their own.

Based between Hong Kong and New York, I'm always chasing new ideas, commissions, and collaborations. This little corner of the internet is my world — take a look around.
