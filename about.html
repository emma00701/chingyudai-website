About me

Hi, I'm Ching Yu Dai, also known as Emma — an artist and maker working across painting, drawing, and three-dimensional sculpture.

My work explores memory, perspective, and the emotional weight of everyday spaces. I'm drawn to expressive color, intricate detail, and materials that can carry a story of their own.

Based between Hong Kong and New York, I'm always interested in new ideas, commissions, and collaborations.
